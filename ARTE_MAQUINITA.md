# Maquinita de Pac-Man

Asset final: `assets/maquinita-pacman.png`.
Generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes, con fondo transparente,
a partir de las dos fotografías de la máquina y el salón como referencia de estilo.

Se utiliza como un sprite independiente en el renderizador del juego. La imagen
original del salón se conserva. Posición y base: `ARCADE` en `engine.js`; dibujo:
`drawArcade` en `game.js`. El evento `arcade` queda preparado para conectar el juego
educativo cuando se proporcione su código.

## Prompt de generación

Single standalone arcade cabinet sprite for a top-down pixel art classroom RPG.
Reproduce the user's upright Pac-Man machine: warm yellow sides and lower front,
red edge trim, black recessed screen bezel and control deck, white marquee with
text PAC-MAN, colored ghosts, white buttons and white ball joystick. Show a small
blue maze with yellow dots on the screen. Face downward toward the viewer with a
small visible right side and an elevated viewpoint matching the classroom's 2.5D
furniture. Crisp pixel art, restrained shading, warm saturated 16-bit palette,
dark pixel outlines. Transparent background; single complete cabinet; no cat,
phone interface, room, floor, product watermark or extra characters.

## Prompt final de recorte

Keep the cabinet exactly as it is: same PAC-MAN marquee, yellow panels, red
outlines, black screen, blue maze, white joystick and buttons, same pose,
proportions, colors and pixel details. Remove all outer halo, vignette, glow,
background and outer cast shadow. Only the cabinet may remain visible. Preserve
black pixels inside the cabinet. Transparent outside the crisp cabinet silhouette,
solid opaque cabinet interior. No feathered edges, glow, blur or extra items;
complete cabinet with transparent margin.
